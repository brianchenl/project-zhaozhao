## Basis for the verdict

A Guam prisoner-of-war photograph is catalogued as showing the aftermath of Hirohito’s surrender broadcast on 15 August 1945. A separate photograph at Lae records personnel reading a surrender-news special edition that day. These records support the circulation of the news, not the signing of the formal instrument. [Source 1](#source-1), [Source 2](#source-2)

The signature block in the instrument published by the U.S. National Archives gives 2 September. A separate film catalogue likewise dates the signing to that day. [Source 3](#source-3), [Source 4](#source-4)

## Scope of this check

This question concerns only the date of signing the formal instrument. The August broadcast audio was not checked, and no broadcast quotations or precise transmission times are given. The interpretation of the two photographs relies on museum descriptions. Individual theatres’ surrender arrangements and the cessation of fighting lie outside this check.
